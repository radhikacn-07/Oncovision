Biomarker & Pathology Reference

Quick context for fields used in the Clinical Assessment workflow.

ER / PR status

Estrogen and progesterone receptor results indicate whether tumor cells express these hormone receptors. Results contribute to tumor biology assessment and treatment planning alongside stage, pathology and patient factors.

HER2 status

HER2 testing assesses overexpression or amplification of the HER2 pathway in tumor tissue. Interpretation depends on validated pathology methods and should be correlated with the complete pathology report.

Ki-67 proliferation index

Ki-67 is a marker of cellular proliferation reported as the proportion of stained tumor cells. Its interpretation and cut-offs can vary by laboratory and clinical context, so it should not be used in isolation.

Histologic grade

Histologic grade describes how tumor architecture and cellular features compare with normal breast tissue. It provides prognostic context but is distinct from the anatomic TNM stage.

Using OncoVision

Decision-support principles for safe clinical use.

1

Correlate every AI output with the clinical examination, original imaging, pathology and relevant prior studies.

2

Treat Grad-CAM as a visualization of model attention—not a confirmed lesion boundary or malignancy location.

3

Review image quality and model confidence before interpreting the output; uncertainty requires qualified clinical review.

4

Use predictions to support workflow prioritization and discussion, never to replace radiologist or treating-physician judgment.

OncoVision is a research decision-support prototype. Final interpretation and care decisions remain with qualified clinicians.
